from @mediapipe/tasks-vision 0.10.35, the browser build of the MediaPipe Tasks
Vision library, unchanged, taken from

face_landmarker.task is the MediaPipe Face Landmarker bundle, version 1,
float16, from
https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/face_landmarker/face_landmarker/float16/1/face_landmarker.task

The model card of the bundle is MODELCARD.md, from
https://huggingface.co/litert-community/MediaPipe-FaceLandmarker
It describes the three networks inside face_landmarker.task: BlazeFace short
range detection, FaceMesh V2 with 478 landmarks, and Blendshape V2.

The wasm binary is the SIMD build. A browser without wasm SIMD can not open the
landmarker; the client then keeps its colour based face patch, so no mask is
lost, only the landmarks.
