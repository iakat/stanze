Jitsi Meet

Secure and high quality meetings

Start a meeting, share the link and talk. Audio and video stay inside the browsers.

Pick a name other people can guess, or share the link of the room.

Every room is sealed end to end in the browser with X25519 + ML-KEM-768 and AES-256-GCM. Compare the emoji code with the other people to rule out a swapped key.

Choose the camera and the microphone before the join. The names appear after the browser has asked once, and the choice is remembered.
