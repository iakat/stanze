- Make a posted URL clickable in the chat: a guest who types or pastes a link gets a clickable link, http and https only, with the target shown on hover. A link is not a preview: no page is fetched and no image is loaded until a guest clicks it, because a link that phones home tells the other side who reads the room (coderobe 21:10, 21:13).
- P2P file offer in the chat, not an upload: a guest drops a file into the chat window and every participant gets an inline offer with the name and the size. A guest who accepts gets the bytes through the existing WebRTC data channel, peer to peer, and nothing goes to the server or the disk of another peer. A guest can refuse, the sender sees who accepted, and a file too big for the data channel is cut into chunks with a progress bar and a cancel (katia 21:13, coderobe 21:13).
- Add a sound switch: the four cues and the lost connection chime are always on, they can only be made quiet by hand. Asked by ax, katia put it at the bottom of the queue (22:38).

A click on a guest is a spotlight: that guest goes to the large area and every other guest goes to the rail (katia 23:36, coderobe 23:36).
